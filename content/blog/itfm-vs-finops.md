# ITFM vs FinOps: What Is the Difference?

IT Financial Management and FinOps both deal with the economics of technology, but they developed to solve different problems.

ITFM generally focuses on the broader financial management of enterprise technology.

FinOps developed primarily around the challenge of managing variable cloud consumption.

## What is ITFM?

IT Financial Management focuses on areas such as:

- technology budgeting
- forecasting
- cost allocation
- cost modelling
- showback and chargeback
- financial transparency
- technology investment planning

Its scope can include infrastructure, applications, employees, vendors, software and cloud services.

## What is FinOps?

FinOps focuses heavily on the financial management and optimization of cloud consumption.

It brings together engineering, finance and business teams to improve accountability for variable technology spending.

Common FinOps activities include:

- cloud cost visibility
- optimization
- commitments and reservations
- unit economics
- forecasting
- ownership and accountability

## The key difference

A useful simplification is:

**ITFM asks how the organization financially manages technology.**

**FinOps asks how teams create accountability and optimize variable technology consumption.**

The boundaries are becoming less clear as both disciplines evolve.

## Where ITFM and FinOps overlap

Both disciplines care about:

- cost visibility
- forecasting
- allocation
- optimization
- accountability
- financial reporting

A large organization may therefore use both approaches rather than choosing one.

## How CIOs can use both

ITFM can provide the broader financial model for technology.

FinOps can provide deeper practices around variable consumption and cloud economics.

Together they can give leadership both enterprise-level financial governance and detailed operational cost management.
