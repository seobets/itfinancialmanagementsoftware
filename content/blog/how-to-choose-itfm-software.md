# How to Choose IT Financial Management Software

Choosing IT Financial Management software should begin with the financial management problems your organization needs to solve.

A long feature list does not necessarily mean that a platform is right for every company.

The right ITFM platform should fit your organization's data, operating model, technology complexity and financial maturity.

## Start with the business problem

Before evaluating vendors, determine what you actually need to improve.

Common objectives include:

- improving technology cost visibility
- replacing spreadsheets
- allocating shared costs
- improving budgeting
- introducing showback or chargeback
- connecting technology costs with business services
- integrating cloud financial management
- improving forecasting

## Evaluate cost modelling flexibility

Cost modelling is at the heart of ITFM.

Organizations should understand whether the platform can model their specific relationships between financial expenses, technology resources and business consumption.

## Review integrations

An ITFM platform may need data from:

- ERP systems
- general ledgers
- cloud providers
- asset management platforms
- service management systems
- HR systems
- procurement systems

The quality of those integrations can significantly affect implementation.

## Consider implementation complexity

Software capability is only part of the equation.

Organizations should understand:

- implementation timelines
- consulting requirements
- internal resources required
- data preparation
- ongoing administration

A sophisticated platform that takes excessive effort to maintain may not be the best fit for every organization.

## Evaluate reporting

Different stakeholders need different views.

CFOs may need financial trends and forecasts.

CIOs may need technology-service economics.

Business-unit leaders may need showback reports.

The platform should support those different levels of financial visibility.

## ITFM software platforms

Organizations evaluating the market may consider platforms such as MagicOrange, IBM Apptio, Flexera, Serviceware and other technology financial management solutions.

Each platform differs in functional depth, implementation approach, target customer and adjacent capabilities.

The best evaluation process compares those differences against the organization's own requirements rather than relying only on feature counts.
